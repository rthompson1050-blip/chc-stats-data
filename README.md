# Cherry Heat Check: Mountain West 2026-27 standings & player stats data

JSON feed behind the standings and team stats pages on [cherryheatcheck.com](https://www.cherryheatcheck.com).

- `data/mw_standings_2026-27.json`: conference and overall records for the 10 Mountain West teams.
- `data/teams/{slug}_2026-27.json`: season player totals per team (per-game numbers and percentages are computed by the site).

**Sources:** official box scores only (team athletic sites, themw.com, NCAA statistics). Exhibitions are not included. Rosters from official team rosters. Updated automatically after each final; numbers are zero until a team's first official game.

Compiled by Cherry Heat Check.
